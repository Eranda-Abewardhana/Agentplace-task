## Business and positioning

SHWASH presents itself as a mobile detailing service that brings equipment and supplies to a customer's home, office, apartment, or parking area. The Austin page says no water or power hookup is needed. The brand's stated value proposition combines convenience with professional processes, trained and vetted detailers, consistent communication, transparent pricing, and a satisfaction guarantee.

The company says it was founded in Austin and serves the Austin, Dallas, and Houston metro areas. This app is oriented to the requested Austin experience; the website's broader Texas reach is represented in copy without implying every listed suburb has the same availability.

Useful trust details published on SHWASH's site include fully insured and licensed service in Texas, trained/background-checked detailers, a 4.9 Google rating, a satisfaction guarantee, at-location service, and the no-water/no-power requirement for customers. Use trust claims selectively and keep them attributed to SHWASH. Review totals and ratings can change.

- The site typography includes Proxima Nova and Inter. The application uses an Inter-led sans-serif system stack to retain the site's clean, practical character without depending on an unavailable licensed font file.

- The current navigation highlights online scheduling and phone/text contact. Its service pages explain on-site convenience before pricing, then show package inclusions, add-ons, and recurring plans.

## Booking, payment, and status boundaries

- Marketing-site scheduling controls open a booking modal. The official booking application is hosted at `https://book.shwash.co/` and displays a JavaScript-required application. Its external workflow is the verified destination.
- The marketing site says the official booking flow asks customers for vehicle size, service, and preferred date/time. No supported query-string or API prefill contract was verified, so this app does not claim it can pass selections into the booking application.
- SHWASH's FAQ says it accepts major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay; payment is processed after service is completed to the customer's satisfaction. Service-plan cards are charged on their billing cycle.
- SHWASH's Dallas blog also describes optional prepayment and other payment methods. That is not proof of an Austin prepayment URL or payment provider. This app follows the Austin/official FAQ and does not generate a payment link or collect payment details.
- Booking confirmation and payment success happen in SHWASH's booking/payment systems. Opening the external site, clicking a client-side button, or returning to this app is not proof of confirmation. This app only reports local preparation/handoff stages and asks the customer to rely on SHWASH's confirmation.
- The official site header shows a call number `+1 512-759-6337` and text number `+1 512-764-3015`. These are kept as separate contact actions.

## Customer needs and evidence-based personas

These are needs-based design personas inferred from the published service model and typical detailing decisions; they are not claims about SHWASH's actual customer demographics.

1. **The schedule-first customer** — needs a clean car but doesn't want to drive to a shop or wait. Wants a home/work/apartment visit, clear time expectations, and an obvious scheduling handoff.
2. **The cabin reset customer** — has stains, odor, kid mess, or pet hair and needs help deciding whether standard interior detailing is enough or a specific extraction/add-on is needed. Wants condition-sensitive guidance without judgment.
3. **The upkeep planner** — values predictable repeat care and wants to compare monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly, and one-time prices. Needs the three-service minimum explained before selecting a plan.
4. **The careful comparer** — wants the price before sharing contact details. Needs vehicle-size categories, package inclusions, add-on costs, and an honest note when SHWASH must quote the vehicle or condition.

No age, income, occupation, or household composition is assumed. Needs are represented as customer goals, not demographic facts.

## Customer expectations in this category

Customers evaluating mobile detailing need to know where the team can work, whether the customer must supply utilities, which surfaces and treatments are included, whether stains/pet hair cost extra, how size affects the price, whether recurring service has a minimum commitment, how long service takes, and what step actually confirms the appointment. These needs directly inform the order of information and handoff language in the app.

## Competitive visual review

The reviewed Austin providers use prominent vehicle/detailing photos, direct service menus, service-by-service inclusions, starting-price labels, and booking actions near prices. Recurring plans are often separated from one-time work. Porsche's model gallery uses large image-led editorial panels and keeps model/category facts close to the image; Lucid uses ample whitespace and product-family navigation; Polestar places shopping tools and ownership/service paths near the top-level experience. These are references for hierarchy and premium automotive imagery only. SHWASH's own aqua identity and service model take priority over their colors, typography, and page designs.

## Design recommendations

1. Keep SHWASH aqua and navy as primary identity cues, with white and pale cool gray for clarity.
2. Use high-quality, context-specific vehicle/interior/detailing imagery. Prefer approved SHWASH photos when the image must depict its actual team/van; use royalty-free editorial photography for illustrative detailing moments and label any showcase as illustrative.
3. Lead with the at-your-location value proposition, then make service choices and vehicle-size pricing easy to scan.
4. Put price conditions and plan minimums next to the relevant price, not in hidden fine print.
5. Make stains and pet hair easy to discuss without shame, and recommend only relevant add-ons.
6. Let the assistant ask a few progressive questions and calculate only from structured official data.
7. Separate recommendation, quote estimate, collected booking details, external booking handoff, confirmation, and post-service payment states.
8. Offer booking summary copy and keep the official booking link highly visible; state plainly that the external booking application does not expose a verified prefill integration to this app.
